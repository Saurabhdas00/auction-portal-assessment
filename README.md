# Auction Portal Back Office Executive Assessment
### Hecta Proptech Private Limited

A complete, responsive pre-interview candidate assessment web application designed for evaluating candidates for an **Auction Portal Back Office Executive** role.

---

## 🏛️ Role Overview & Evaluated Competencies

The **Auction Portal Back Office Executive** manages bank auction property listings, verifies official auction notices, coordinates with banks/ARCs/NBFCs, and maintains operational trackers and MIS reports under the SARFAESI framework.

### Evaluated Skills:
1. **Data Accuracy**: Zero-error handling for Reserve Prices, EMD figures, and property IDs.
2. **Excel & Data Handling**: Functions (`SUM`, `ROUND`, `COUNTIFS`, `XLOOKUP`), conditional formatting for duplicates, and data manipulation.
3. **Auction Operations**: SARFAESI timelines, EMD deposit milestones, and bidding checklists.
4. **Document Verification**: Sale notices, corrigendums, encumbrance certificates, and bidder KYC.
5. **Operational Judgment**: Prioritization under tight deadlines, symbolic vs. physical possession, and de-duplication SOPs.

## 📋 Assessment Structure

- **Format**: 15 Practical & Scenario-Based Questions
- **Time Limit**: 30 Minutes (Live Countdown Timer with automatic submission on expiry)
- **Scoring**: Maximum 100 Points
  - **Easy (Q1–Q5)**: 4 points each (Total = 20 pts)
  - **Medium (Q6–Q10)**: 6 points each (Total = 30 pts)
  - **Hard (Q11–Q15)**: 10 points each (Total = 50 pts)
- **Answer Key Benchmark**: 1-A, 2-B, 3-B, 4-B, 5-C, 6-A, 7-A, 8-A, 9-A, 10-A, 11-A, 12-C, 13-A, 14-A, 15-A

## 🌟 Application Features

- **No Personal Info Barrier**: Candidates can click **"Start Test Now"** to begin immediately.
- **Interactive Question Navigator**: Real-time visual states for *Answered*, *Unanswered*, *Current*, and *Flagged* questions.
- **Submission Confirmation**: Pre-submission breakdown with answered counts and warning prompts if questions remain unanswered.
- **Two-Card Personalized Completion Screen**:
  - Uncropped, original aspect ratio sunflower/cat hero image (`sunflower-cat.png`).
  - Warm cream background with subtle sparkle/sunflower animations.
  - Dynamically calculated score (`{SCORE}/100`), accuracy percentage, and time taken.
  - Warm interview encouragement: *"All the best for your interview! 💛 — You've got this. Wishing you the very best!"*.
- **Detailed Results Analysis**:
  - Section scores (Easy /20, Medium /30, Hard /50).
  - 5 Skill breakdown progress bars with descriptive feedback.
  - Question-by-question review with green/red status badges, candidate selections, correct answers, and operational explanations.
  - Platform disclaimer notice.
- **Evaluator / Admin Dashboard**:
  - Passcode: `admin123`.
  - Metrics: Total candidates, completed assessments, average score, highest score, average completion time.
  - Search, filter by score range & experience, and sort.
  - Full candidate answer dossier viewer.
  - **CSV Export**: Downloads a complete report formatted for operational MIS.
- **LocalStorage Persistence**: Auto-saves every answer and flag in real time; session restores upon browser refresh.
